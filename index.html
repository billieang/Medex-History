Source, attribution and citations

The PREVENT™ equations were developed by select members of the American Heart Association Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic Scientific Advisory Group. The risk equations were derived and validated in a large, diverse sample of over 6 million individuals.

Khan SS et al. Development and Validation of the American Heart Association's PREVENT Equations. Circulation. 2024;149(6):430–449.

Khan SS et al. AHA PREVENT equations: A scientific statement from the American Heart Association. Circulation. 2023;148(24):1982–2004.

R software: Krishnan V, Petito LC, Huang X, Khan SS. (2026). AHAprevent: AHA PREVENT equations for R. R package version 1.1.0.

STATA software: Huang X, Sang Y, Khan SS. (2026). aha_prevent: AHA PREVENT equations for STATA. STATA package version 1.1.0.

The information provided herein is based in part on PREVENT™, which is the American Heart Association's (AHA) online risk calculator for primary prevention patients. The use of the AHA's material is limited to certain aspects of PREVENT™.

The information derived from the use of PREVENT™ is based on PREVENT™ 1.0.0. Updates and future adaptations of PREVENT™ may yield different results and conclusions.

PREVENT was developed and validated using U.S. population data. Its calibration and interpretation in Indonesia have not been established. SDI is based on U.S. ZIP-code socioeconomic data and should not be assigned to Indonesian locations. The SDI and Full variants therefore have a geographic limitation for this user. This tool is for personal informational use, not clinical decision-making.
